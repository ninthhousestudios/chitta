### Astrobench results (reference)

80 queries, 4 slices:

| Config | r@5 | r@10 | r@20 | hit | MRR |
|--------|-----|------|------|-----|-----|
| Dense-only | 80.6% | 90.0% | 94.4% | 96.2% | 0.6517 |
| **Dense+Sparse** | **81.2%** | **93.1%** | **95.6%** | **97.5%** | **0.6953** |
| Dense+Sparse+FTS | 81.2% | 93.1% | 95.6% | 97.5% | 0.6951 |

Per-slice: D (multi-hop) gets biggest sparse boost (+10pp r@5). C (plain language) hit rate jumps 86.7%→93.3%.

### Slice C investigation

Slice C (plain language / paraphrase) is still weakest at 80% r@5. Worth investigating which queries fail and whether query reformulation or chunk strategy changes help.
