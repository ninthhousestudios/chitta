# Frame chitta as a working model of Josh, not a personality system

The chitta-rs rewrite trimmed the Python kitchen-sink memory store to a verbatim, bi-temporal core but kept "memory bank" framing. The 2026-05-07 design discussion narrowed chitta's contract to modeling the human in the loop. Naming candidates included "personality store," "identity store," and "self-model"; "personality" imports Big-Five / MBTI baggage and is narrower than what we store (mental models, working preferences, communication style). Decided: chitta is the **working model of Josh** — what he values, how he works, what he prefers, what mental models he uses. The Sanskrit *citta* (field of impressions / samskāra that conditions future behavior) fits this framing; the implementation had drifted from the name and is being brought back to it. "Personality" is dropped from chitta's vocabulary; "working model" is canonical, "Josh model" is acceptable shorthand.
